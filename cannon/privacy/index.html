Sky Cannon Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

The short version. We do not collect anything. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no servers of ours. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

The game does show advertising, and you choose when. Google's advertising software collects data for that, and the rest of this page is about what it does and how to say no to it.

What the game stores

Your level, coins, upgrades, the three sound switches, and whether you have bought Sky Cannon Pro. All of it is written to a file inside the app on your own device. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

There is no forced advertising. Ads only appear in two places, and only if you tap the button yourself: to revive after losing a level, and to double the coins from a level you have just cleared. You can play the whole game and never see one.

Those ads come from Google AdMob. Google's own page on what its advertising software handles lists the device's advertising identifier, which ads you have seen, taps and video views, app performance data such as launch time, crash logs, and your IP address, which is used to estimate roughly where in the world you are. Google uses these for advertising and for its own analytics.

Google's privacy policy covers what it does with them: policies.google.com/privacy. Google's description of what the software handles is at developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you Google's consent form before any ad is requested. What you choose there is what Google is allowed to do. You can change your answer later from that form.

Tracking and the advertising identifier (IDFA)

Before the first ad is requested, iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. That is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and the answer is yours. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not available to Google and the ads you see are less specific. Nothing else changes: the game plays exactly the same either way, and every reward still works.

You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

Sky Cannon Pro

Sky Cannon Pro is a one-off purchase that removes the advertising: Revive and double coins are simply given to you. The purchase goes through Apple. We never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information from it. Apple tells the game whether you own it, and nothing more.

Children

Sky Cannon is not aimed at children and is not published in Apple's Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Your choices, in one place

  • Never tap a reward button, and no ad is ever requested.
  • Say no to the tracking prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings.
  • In the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, change your answer in Google's consent form.
  • Buy Sky Cannon Pro and the advertising stops entirely.
  • Delete the app and everything stored on the device goes with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. There is nothing here to notify you through, so the date is the honest record.
