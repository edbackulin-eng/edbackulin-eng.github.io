How do I play?

Hold your thumb anywhere on the screen and the cannon fires. Slide your thumb and the cannon follows. Break every ball before one of them touches the cannon. The number on a ball is how much health it has left; when it runs out, the ball splits into two smaller ones, until the smallest ones break for good.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on the device and nowhere else, so it does not follow you to a new iPhone and it does not survive deleting the app. There is no account to restore it from. If progress disappeared without the app being deleted, please write to us and say what level you were on; we can tell you whether it is a bug we can fix.

I bought Pro but the ads are still there

Open Settings in the game, tap Restore Purchases, and make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. If it still does not come back, email us the date of the purchase and we will help.

The Revive or double coins button does not appear

Those buttons only appear when an ad is ready. A new app can take a few hours before the advertising network starts filling requests, and a poor connection stops them too. With Sky Cannon Pro the rewards are given straight away, with no ad and no waiting.

I want fewer ads

There is no forced advertising at all: an ad only ever appears if you tap a reward button yourself. Sky Cannon Pro removes them completely.

How do I change the tracking or consent answer?

For tracking, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form can be reopened from the game. Both are explained on the privacy policy page.

No sound

The game respects the silent switch on purpose, so it never interrupts anything. Check the switch, the volume, and the three switches in the game's own Settings.

A level feels impossible

Levels are built from the level number alone, so the level you failed is exactly the same level when you try again; it is not getting harder behind your back. Spend coins on fire rate and power between attempts. If a level still looks wrong, tell us the number and we will play it ourselves.
